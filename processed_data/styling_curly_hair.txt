PRODUCTS & STYLING — CURL-SPECIFIC ADVICE

LOOSER CURLS
PRODUCTS
Lightweight creams are recommended for looser curls. Lightweight gels can provide definition without weighing down the hair. Detanglers can help with managing and preparing the hair. Heavy creams and gels may weigh down looser curls and cause excessive clumping.Strong-hold or “heavy-duty” gels are better suited for updos and hairstyles requiring stronger hold.
Brands like Rizzo's Curls have lightweight products that will help with definition without weighing 3a-3c curly hair down. 

APPLICATION
Apply products to damp hair rather than soaking-wet hair. Damp hair allows products to absorb moisture without excessive dripping. Hair masks can be applied to soaking-wet hair when appropriate.


TIGHTER CURLS
PRODUCTS
Heavier creams and gels may be more suitable for tighter curls, depending on the desired hairstyle. Lightweight gels may help control frizz but may not provide enough hold for lasting curl definition. A lightweight gel can help maintain definition when paired with a heavier curling cream.

Product combinations that may work well include:
Light curling cream + heavier gel
Gel + mousse
Heavier curling cream + lightweight gel
Curling cream + mousse

Brands like Aunt Jackies, Shea Moisture, Carol's Daughter, and African pride's moisture miracle are typically good choices for those with curlier hair textures (3c-4c).
