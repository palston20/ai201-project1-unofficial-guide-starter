HUMIDITY, FRIZZ, AND SHRINKAGE — COMMUNITY ADVICE

TOPIC
Humidity, frizz, shrinkage, curl definition, and maintaining stretched natural hair

HOW HUMIDITY AFFECTS NATURAL HAIR
Humid weather can cause curly and coily hair to lose definition and become more expanded or shrunken.
Some community members describe humidity as making it difficult to maintain a stretched or defined style outdoors.
Several commenters suggest that completely preventing frizz or shrinkage may not be realistic in highly humid environments.
Some commenters recommend accepting or embracing shrinkage and frizz rather than trying to completely prevent them.

STRONG-HOLD STYLING PRODUCTS
Several commenters recommend using strong-hold gels or styling products to help preserve curl definition in humid weather.
Community suggestions include: 
Use a strong-hold gel or styling product.
Choose products intended to provide longer-lasting hold.
Apply styling products while the hair is still damp or wet.
Make sure the hair is well-defined before allowing it to dry.
Avoid disturbing the styling cast once the hair has completely dried, as some commenters believe the cast helps preserve definition.
Anti-humidity sprays may also be used to help maintain a style.

HUMECTANTS AND HUMIDITY
The community has differing opinions about humectants in humid weather. One commenter recommends avoiding products with humectants when trying to maintain definition in humid conditions.
Another commenter recommends embracing humidity and using a humectant. Ingredients identified by commenters as humectants include glycerin, aloe vera, and honey.

STRETCHING THE HAIR
Some commenters recommend using stretched hairstyles instead of trying to maintain a fully defined wash-and-go.
Suggested techniques include:
Twist-outs
Finger coils
Braids or twists used to stretch the hair
Allowing twists to dry completely before taking them down
Using a hooded dryer or carefully drying the hair to improve definition
Retwisting the hair at night to maintain the style
One commenter described using multiple sections for a twist-out, allowing the hair to dry, and separating the twists afterward to create a combination of curl definition, volume, and length.

ANTI-HUMIDITY PRODUCTS
Community members also recommend products specifically marketed for humidity protection.
Suggestions included:
Anti-humidity sprays
Anti-humidity serums
Strong-hold gels
Curl creams or custards designed to provide stronger hold
Specific product recommendations are based on individual experiences and may not work the same way for every hair type.

EMBRACING SHRINKAGE AND FRIZZ
Not all commenters recommend fighting humidity.
Several responses suggest:
Accepting natural shrinkage.
Embracing frizz rather than trying to eliminate it.
Allowing the hair to respond naturally to humid weather.
Recognizing that maintaining completely defined curls in humid conditions may be unrealistic.
