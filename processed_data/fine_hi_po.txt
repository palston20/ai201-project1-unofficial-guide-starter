REDDIT USER — FINE, HIGH-POROSITY CURLY HAIR ROUTINE
HAIR TYPE
Fine, high-porosity curly hair.

CLEANSING
Cleansing conditioner may be too heavy for fine hair.
Consider using a clarifying shampoo or low-poo shampoo instead.
Less product may be more effective for fine hair.

MOISTURE
High-porosity hair can benefit from heavier creams and oils, but using too many moisturizing products at once may be unnecessary.
When hair is very dry, use one moisturizing hair mask rather than layering multiple heavy products.
On less-dry wash days, a rinse-out conditioner followed by a small amount of leave-in conditioner may be sufficient.
POST-WASH STYLING

A “one-and-done” approach to styling products has worked better for the user than layering 2–3 products.
For dryness: use a small amount of leave-in conditioner.
For volume or definition: use either mousse OR curl cream, depending on the desired result.
Avoid automatically using multiple styling products simply because they are common in curly-hair routines.

OIL
Use only a drop or two of oil on the ends of the hair.
Apply oil only when the hair is completely dry.
The user reports that this helps keep their ends shiny and healthy.

GENERAL APPROACH
Use a light hand when applying products.
Keep the routine simple and consistent.
Be patient when determining what works for fine, high-porosity hair.
The user's overall approach is to avoid product buildup while still providing enough moisture for high-porosity hair.
