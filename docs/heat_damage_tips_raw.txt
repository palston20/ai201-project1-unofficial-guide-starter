You can continue to do protein or bond builder treatments but you need a major haircut. You can gradually cut and do styles to hide the heat damage or cut all your hair off and start over. What do you feel comfortable with? Could you cut at least 2-4 inches off now? You could do styles like braidouts, twistouts, or set your hair with perm rods or flexirods. I am sorry your hair got damaged it was so beautiful but it will grow back healthy again! As a rule of thumb when I use heat, I use 2 different types of heat protectant. One is a liquid or light milk and the second one is a serum. I also towel dry with a microfiber towel so I am not blowdryong soaking wet hair and do so on medium heat. I don't flatiron but if I did I would instead blow dry on high but my flatiron would not go above 375.

I also forgot to add that you can play with kinky curly or kinky straight wigs. I prefer headband ones because it is less tension on my edges and my hair is cornrowed underneath.

Using heat protectant is not enough. When flat ironing coarser hair types, bond builders need to be incorporated into the shampooing & conditioning process to prep the hair. The hair needs to be deep conditioned with steam/heat, & heat protectant needs to be layered.

You need a heat protectant for the blowdry phase (leave in, blow dry spray, & serum need to have heat protection) & you need to apply a heat protectant when the hair is dry (it's called thermal protectant & needs to be for dry hair) before flat ironing. DON'T spray the thermal spray all over your hair in a cloud, spray it in sections & proceed with flat ironing.

Finally the heat protectant needs to protect up to 450 Degrees. It can't just say "Heat protectant" as it won't be powerful enough for your hair. Don't flat iron your hair on 450, The iron shouldn't exceed 410, however the protectants that go up to 450 are the gold standard.

You need to use a bond builder primarily & a protein treatment secondarily bc based on these pics, the heat has damaged your disulfide bonds which is why it's straight. When doing a healthy silkpress, only the hydrogen bonds are broken but when you wash your hair they come back together giving you curls. In your case, they did not, so them disulfide bonds are the ones that you need to fix.

Protein treatments are good bc they will add elasticity back to your hair, but they can be too much & cause overload/breakage. Your hair will be back right in 6 months if you continue to wash it & give it treatments. Don't put anymore heat on your hair & the curls should return. Next time you decide to flat iron your hair, get the proper products & take better precautions next time.
