so the trick is to use a strong hold gel or styling product that doesn’t have glycerin in it or any ingredients that are humectants (aloe vera, honey, and silicones to name a few)

Humectants draws in moisture from the air into your hair

Another tip is to not break the cast on your hair after its fully dry (the cast will keep your hair intact and defined longer)

Lastly make sure your hair looks defined and not frizzy before you dry it (rather its air drying or sitting under a hooded dryer)

These work for me and i moved to a humid city been here for two years hope this helps 🫶🏾

i have mainly 4c with 4b in the back, miss jessie’s leave in condish & coily custard worked wonders for me against super humidity!

the other alternative is just doing a lil twist out! that’s my go to fro style now. after you wash and detangle, add moisturizer of your choice to wet/damp hair (i use carol’s daughter’s coco creme coil enhancing moisture butter) & oil of choice to seal moisture (i’ve been experimenting with dif ones so whatever u feel is most compatible with your hair). add the products to each part on in its own for efficient product distribution (i make 6 to 10 parts, doesn’t have to be even or neat) takes me like 20 min. let air dry for a day or sit under dryer head/safely blow dry for more defined coils. then take out and voila! use a hairpick at the roots to add volume to hair and the hairstyle is a mix of curls & coils with length/volume since the twists stretch the hair naturally. and at EOD just retwist it again, retwisting takes like 10 min for me tops. refresh with products as you see fit. best of luck ✨
