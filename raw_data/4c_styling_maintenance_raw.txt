You have gorgeous hair
Handling 4C hair shouldn’t be made harder than it is. A lot of the videos we find online may be for looser curl patterns than yours, use a lot more product than you have access to or be made by people who have done this longer than you have. Makes the entire process seem intimidating. Start real simple with your styling and products. After you get the hang of detangling your hair n moisturizing it, you’ll slowly ease yourself into styling it. Start with the simple n convenient pineapple like you have. Then move on to the two lil puffs. Slowly you’ll find yourself getting more excited and more comfortable trying other hairstyles. Even the complicated ones. Avoid tight hairstyles or too much product as that will damage your hair over time and create buildup. Also use water a lot during detangling.
