Sweatband and pull my twists/braids up to a high ponytail or bun. I wash once a week and put mousse on it before I tie it down every night. I usually can get a month's worth of my twists from just my natural hair (I don't add hair to them or my knotless braids anymore. )

Edge control can also work on laying your hair down, if your concerned about frizz and fly-aways. My hair grows considerably fast too so a month is the most I can really do with them, but I don't mind it being a little messy.
